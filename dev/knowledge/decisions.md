# Decisions

## One model, three drawings
Dynamics (system dynamics), Blocks (control), Process (process map) stay separate, each in its discipline's traditional
style. New ideas are shown by modelling them, not by new UI. *Areas: diagram, view, page, panel, ui, layout, drawing.*

## Interventions are evidence of missing loops
Alerts, approvals and notifications are counted as a stock that should trend down; suppressing one is never counted as
a fix, and a temporary rise while a loop is built is fine. *Areas: intervention, alert, approval, notification, inbox.*

## Human approval is for a system's early stages
The approval ladder moves classes from a person to AI to a program as their record earns it. An AI approver needs a
sound positive allowlist of what it may approve (a denylist guard was bypassed). *Areas: approval, approver, ladder,
allowlist, gate, guard, inbox.*

## Model changes are judged, then reviewed
The judge tests by the record; structure and redesigns also get a reviewer's reading. Nothing structural is written
without approval unless the owner names the class `auto`. *Areas: modelling, judge, improver, redesign, redesigner.*

## Redesign runs on its own
One system at a time, bounded by rests, turns and a daily cap that fails closed. *Areas: redesign, redesigner.*

## Honest numbers over flattering ones
Projections say "low confidence" when the model fits badly; benchmarks report what the evidence supports. *Areas:
projection, estimate, benchmark, confidence.*

## Daemons over a process per item
Where a program stays up; the AI steps stay one process per run so their cost is measured per run. *Areas: daemon.*

## The factory starts small
One implementor, one reviewer; pools rise only after a streak of good results. *Areas: factory, pool.*

## Security effort goes where code nobody vouched for runs
The sandbox, repair. For the owner's own tooling, review for correctness and these rules rather than hardening.
*Areas: sandbox, repair, security, guard, container.*
