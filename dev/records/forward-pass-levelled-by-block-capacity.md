# forward-pass-levelled-by-block-capacity: Before the estimates, and at a moment travelled to, the Timeline's forward pass is levelled by each block's capacity, read from one shared function

depends-on: [eta-works-out-on-the-factory-record]

Problem: ergostat/precedence.py's projection (cpm in build) is the critical path method with no resources. It is drawn on every first paint before the estimates exist, at every moment travelled to (SPEC §5a: estimates are of now only) and whenever they fail. In it every unblocked or queued item starts now: on the factory record 103 of 112 gaps, 'done in about 7.6h' for about 110 gaps of median 3.2 h each. This is the owner's 'as if 100 gap runs start at once'. Capacity is read today only inside etamodel.read, which fails on the factory record until eta-works-out-on-the-factory-record lands (hence the dependency).
Change: (1) one function etamodel.capacities(project, snapshot) -> {block: {lanes, source}} is the only place a block's simulated capacity is decided (pool, or pool now from its stock, the agent's at-once, the daemon's concurrency, whichever binds), worked out once per snapshot and kept with it (never per request); eta.py and precedence.py both call it. eta-refused-takes-are-a-closed-gate later makes this one function return the measured concurrency, so the levelled pass and the simulation change together. (2) a deterministic levelled schedule in precedence.py, the serial schedule-generation scheme of resource-constrained scheduling: in precedence order, least slack first then longest path ahead (the dispatcher's tie-break), each item is placed at the earliest time its predecessors are done and the block that takes it from its state has a free lane; durations are the kinds' medians as now. (3) the projected bars, out['end'] and the legend's 'done in about' come from the levelled schedule. The unlevelled chain is still marked but is labelled for what it is: 'longest chain of waits, ignoring capacity'; the finish drawn and said is the levelled end, so the two never read as one claim (this amends filed Q60's 'agrees with the critical path': its clock time is the levelled, later the simulated, finish). (4) every time printed by the Timeline uses filed Q34's formatter (date and zone when not today). The legend says 'levelled by each block's capacity, median times; the simulation replaces it when worked out'.
Definition of done:
1. 100 unblocked items on a block of capacity 6 with a median of 20 min end at about 100/6 x 20 min, not 20 min.
2. No block has more lanes busy than its capacity in the levelled bars; dependencies are respected.
3. A moment travelled to shows levelled bars.
4. The levelling costs under 50 ms on 600 items, with capacities read from the snapshot's kept table (no record read in precedence.build).
5. The marked chain's label contains 'ignoring capacity' and the legend's finish equals the last levelled bar's end.
6. SPEC §5a The critical path describes the levelled projection and the label.
Files: ergostat/numbers/etamodel.py (capacities), ergostat/numbers/eta.py (calls it), ergostat/precedence.py, ergostat/web/static/relations.js, tests/test_precedence.py, tests/test_items_page.py, docs/SPEC.md.
Acceptance tests: test_precedence.py::test_forward_pass_levelled_by_capacity, ::test_levelled_respects_dependencies, ::test_capacity_comes_from_one_function; test_items_page.py::test_timeline_first_paint_is_levelled (on the roadmap fixture before estimates arrive no more than the developer block's 2 bars overlap).

## Where it stopped

Built, round 1: every acceptance test is written and passes, and so does a timing test (`test_levelling_600_items_is_quick`, marked serial). Net 109 lines outside tests and docs, against a budget of 110. Choices made without anyone to ask, each the most reversible one:

- eta.py itself is unchanged. The simulation gets its capacity through `etamodel.read`, which builds each `BlockIn` from `capacities()` (`BlockIn.lanes`, which `capacity()` returns unless a what-if pool is set). `test_capacity_comes_from_one_function` checks that a change made in `capacities` reaches `eta.simulate`'s `limit` and the levelled pass. `read` passes its own `{"at": at}` as the snapshot (the backtest reads past moments). The Timeline passes the server's snapshot (`relations_api.graph`), which keeps the table as `snapshot["capacities"]`. `relations_api.py` is not in the Files list, but it is where the snapshot reaches `precedence.build` (5 lines).
- Which block a state belongs to: a state a block takes from is that block's, a working state belongs to the block that took it from the queue before it, and a hold belongs to its holder. Each block is levelled on its own capacity. Agent seats and daemon runs shared between blocks are not counted together (the simulation does count them). SPEC says so.
- `es` and `ef` in the answer are now the levelled start and end. `ls`, `lf`, `slack` and `critical` stay the unlevelled CPM's, and so does the dispatcher's `order_ready`: it calls `build` without `lanes`, so its order is unchanged.
- With the estimates present, the legend's p50 still never reads before `end`, which is now the levelled end. This keeps the existing test's floor.
- The legend says "the simulation replaces it when worked out" only while the estimates are pending (now, not a snapshot file). At a moment travelled to it says the levelled sentence without that clause, because no simulation comes for a past moment.
- "Q34's formatter": no filed Q34 is in this clone. The Timeline's own `rgClock` (date when not today) gained the zone when not today, and the legend, bar titles and whiskers now use it. The axis ticks keep their short labels (said in SPEC).
- The page test holds the estimates back by asking each `items-graph` with a what-if that names no block. That answer carries no estimates, so the bars are the levelled pass every time. The test marks the answer as pending. Breaking the levelling (99 lanes for every block) made all three unit acceptance tests and the page test fail; restored.

Round 2:

- Merged main (73d266e7). The conflict in relations.js was the legend. Main's two sentences are kept: "the simulation failed: …" and "the simulation is being worked out". Main's "unlevelled" clause is dropped, because the bars are now levelled. The branch's levelled sentence follows them. "Being worked out" now shows while `eta_pending` is set, the same condition as the "replaces it" clause, instead of main's clock test.
- The blocking finding (a queue wait held a lane) is fixed. A state a block takes from is now a `WAIT` step that holds no lane. `level` takes the lane at the wait's median end, or later if no lane is free by then. The levelling's wait replaces the median's; the two are never added. SPEC §5a now says this. New test `test_queue_wait_holds_no_lane`: a reviewer of capacity 1 takes at `review`, the queue's median is 2 h, and the end is 4 h 20. With the old mapping restored, the test fails at 94,800 s (about 26 h, the double count). With the median-wait floor removed, it fails at 8,400 s. Both breaks were restored.
- A wait spanning several queue states in a row is drawn as one bar, named after its first state.
Overrun reason: net 129 lines against 110. The blocking finding needs the queue wait to hold no lane and the take to be levelled from where the wait began. That takes the lane search out of `level` as a function called twice, plus the `WAIT` step, about 20 lines that the round-1 count (109) had no room for.
